Getting Around Maheshwar

Whether you're riding solo or with family, find easy local transport options across town.

🛺

Auto Rickshaws

Hire for quick trips to ghats, markets, and temples at fixed fares.

💰 ₹30–₹100 per trip
🛵

Bike Rentals

Rent scooters or bicycles for flexible and fun sightseeing.

💰 ₹250–₹400/day
🚕

Local Taxis

Book cabs for day trips to Mandleshwar, Omkareshwar or Jam Gate.

💰 ₹500–₹1200
🚌

Public Buses

Budget travel via government buses connecting to Indore, Barwaha.

💰 ₹20–₹80
🚲

Cycle Rickshaws

Eco-friendly rides through narrow lanes and ghat alleys.

💰 ₹20–₹50
🚐

Guided Tour Vans

Pre-booked vans with guides for families and large groups.

💰 ₹1000–₹2000
