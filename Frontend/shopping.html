Shopping in Maheshwar

Take home a piece of Maheshwar — from handwoven sarees to local souvenirs and spiritual keepsakes.

Rehwa Society

Famous for authentic Maheshwari sarees, made by women weavers in traditional styles.

Ahilya Ghat Market

Find brassware, souvenirs, pooja items, and traditional wear at local stalls.

Main Bazaar Road

Vibrant local street shops with textiles, toys, bangles, and snacks.

Loom View Shop

Watch live weaving and purchase fresh-off-the-loom sarees directly from artisans.

Souvenir Corners

Pick up magnets, diaries, postcards, and spiritual tokens with local prints.

Royal Handloom House

Premium handloom collections and custom weaving options for gifting and occasions.
