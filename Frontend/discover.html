Discover More in Maheshwar

Beyond the famous fort and ghats, Maheshwar is home to hidden gems, cultural heritage, scenic vistas, and spiritual peace. Explore what more this charming town offers.

Ahilya Fort

The majestic riverside fort of Queen Ahilyabai Holkar, now a heritage hotel and museum.

Ahilya Ghat

Perfect spot for a peaceful boat ride, evening aarti, and river meditation.

Maheshwari Weaving

Visit local looms and see how the famous Maheshwari sarees are handcrafted.

Rajwada & Ancient Temples

Explore the royal palace complex and ancient Shiva temples carved in stone.

Narmada Sunset Point

Watch the sun dip below the ghats, reflecting golden hues on the sacred river.

Boat Ride Experience

Take a serene ride on the Narmada River and see Maheshwar's beauty from the water.
