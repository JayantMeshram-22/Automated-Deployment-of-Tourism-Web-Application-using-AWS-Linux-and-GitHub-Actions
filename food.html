Famous Local Food Centers in Maheshwar

Taste the authentic flavors of Maheshwar at these beloved local spots known for traditional Malwa cuisine.

Laxmi Bhojanalaya

Simple veg meals with dal, baati, and churma. Loved by locals for over 25 years.

⭐ 4.6/5 💰 ₹100–₹200

Shree Krishna Bhojanalaya

Authentic thali with unlimited servings. Must try their kadhi and jeera rice.

⭐ 4.5/5 💰 ₹120–₹250

Maharaja Dhaba

Spicy local curries, parathas, and lassi served fresh and hot. Highway-style seating.

⭐ 4.3/5 💰 ₹80–₹180

River View Snacks

Enjoy poha, jalebi, and chai with a serene Narmada view in the morning hours.

⭐ 4.7/5 💰 ₹30–₹70

Sarvottam Malwa Bhoj

Best place for traditional Malwa dishes like bhutte ka kees and khichdi.

⭐ 4.4/5 💰 ₹150–₹300

Saraswati Dining Hall

Locals’ favorite for clean, home-style meals and seasonal sabjis. Very affordable.

⭐ 4.2/5 💰 ₹80–₹150
